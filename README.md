# Hospitality Uniforms & Custom Embroidery — WordPress Demo

This branch implements the supplied client brief as a real WordPress theme plus a quote-management plugin.

## Included
- Five required pages: Home, Services, Hospitality Solutions, About, Contact / Get a Quote
- Exact deep navy (#0A2540) and gold (#C9A227) visual direction
- Hospitality-uniform-focused imagery only
- Mobile-responsive navigation and layouts
- Custom embroidery, logo digitization, branded apparel and easy reordering sections
- Role-based hospitality solutions for kitchen, front-of-house, hotel, cafe/bar and catering teams
- Quote form with name, business, phone, email, garment checkboxes, quantity, logo upload and notes
- Quote requests stored in WordPress admin
- Owner email notification via wp_mail
- Honeypot and basic rate limiting for spam protection
- Business phone, email, service area and WhatsApp settings in the WordPress Customizer
- Basic page meta descriptions
- Elementor installed by the Playground blueprint for optional later page-builder conversion

## Production notes
The brief does not provide the client's final business name, logo, phone, email, service area, domain or hosting account. The demo therefore uses a neutral hospitality-uniform brand and clearly marked contact placeholders. Replace those once the client supplies them. Production email delivery should use the hosting provider's mail service or an SMTP plugin.
